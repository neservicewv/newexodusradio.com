Six areas of readiness

Practical, safety-focused preparation for your household, held together by faith. Each area has a free guide you can download, print and keep with your supplies.

  • Water preparedness

    Safe drinking water comes first. Plan how your household will store, filter and purify water when the tap stops.

    • Water filtration
    • Water purification
    • Rainwater collection

    • Emergency drinking water
    • Safe storage containers

  • Emergency food storage

    Build a pantry your family will actually eat, and keep it fresh by rotating it into everyday meals.

    Topics

    • Long-term food storage
    • Shelf-stable foods
    • Rice and beans
    • Canned goods
    • Freeze-dried foods
    • Food rotation
    • Safe storage containers
    • Mylar bags and oxygen absorbers
    • Building a household emergency pantry
    PDF guide: Emergency Food StorageLearn more about emergency food storage

  • Emergency power

    Keep lights, phones and essential devices running through an outage, and run backup power safely.

    Topics

    • Portable power stations
    • Solar generators
    • Battery banks
    • Solar panels
    • Emergency lighting
    • Backup charging
    • Generator safety
    PDF guide: Emergency Power and LightingLearn more about emergency power

  • Home preparedness

    Know what your household will do, where you’ll meet, and how you’ll stay in touch, before you need to.

    Topics

    • Emergency evacuation plans
    • Shelter-in-place planning
    • Emergency communication
    • Family meeting locations
    • Emergency supply kits
    • Heating and cooling during outages
    • Fire safety
    PDF guide: Household Emergency Planning and SafetyLearn more about home preparedness

  • Medical preparedness

    Be ready to handle minor injuries and keep important medical information organized if services are interrupted.

    Topics

    • First aid kits
    • Emergency medications
    • Basic first aid education
    • Emergency contact lists
    • Medical supply organization
    • Preparing for interruptions in medical services

    This is general education, not medical advice. Talk with your doctor or pharmacist about medications and health needs, and call 911 in an emergency.

    PDF guide: Household Medical and First Aid PreparednessLearn more about medical preparedness

    Readiness starts with the heart. Stay rooted in Christ, in Scripture and in fellowship, and be ready to serve others.

    Topics

    • Spiritual discernment
    • Biblical perseverance
    • Christian fellowship
    • Helping others during difficult times
    • Trusting God while acting responsibly
