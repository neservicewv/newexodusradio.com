Our mission

We proclaim Jesus Christ, teach the Scriptures, and help believers stay spiritually awake and practically ready, standing together through whatever comes.

Scripture calls God’s people to come out from the corruption of the world. We take that call seriously and hopefully: not with fear, but with faith, preparation and love for one another.

  • Faith

    A genuine, growing relationship with Jesus Christ, and help for those discovering Him for the first time.

    Revelation 14:12

  • Unity

    Respectful conversation among believers from different traditions, centered on the Savior we share.

    Ephesians 4:3–6

  • Truth

    Careful study of Scripture, with clear lines between what the Bible says, what is reported, and what is interpretation.

    2 Corinthians 6:17

  • Preparedness

    Ready in spirit and ready at home: prayer and watchfulness alongside sensible planning for hard days.

    Matthew 25:1–13

Bridging the Gap

One Body.One Savior.One Mission.

Bridging the Gap brings together Christians from different traditions, backgrounds and denominations to listen, study Scripture, and recognize the faith we share.

Believers don’t agree on every theological question. Disagreement doesn’t make respectful conversation impossible.

Faith doesn’t mean we shouldn’t prepare.

The Preparedness Center offers biblical wisdom, practical knowledge, and responsible preparation for the challenges life may bring.

  • Water

    Storage, filtration, purification and conservation for safe drinking water in an emergency.

  • Food storage

    Shelf-stable foods, rotation, safe containers and building a household emergency pantry.

  • Emergency power

    Power stations, solar, battery banks, lighting and generator safety.

  • Home

    Evacuation and shelter-in-place plans, family meeting points, supply kits and fire safety.

  • Medical

    First aid kits, basic first aid education, and organizing supplies and contacts.

  • Spiritual

    Prayer, Scripture study, discernment, fellowship and caring for others in hard times.

Preparedness content is educational. It doesn’t replace guidance from medical professionals or your local emergency management office.
