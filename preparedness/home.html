Home preparedness

Know what your household will do, where you’ll meet, and how you’ll stay in touch, before you need to.

Proverbs 22:3

What this guide covers

  • Emergency evacuation plans

    Knowing your routes out, what to take, and what to do before you leave.

  • Shelter-in-place planning

    Choosing a safe room and preparing to stay put when that’s the safer choice.

  • Emergency communication

    How your family will reach each other when phones are down, including an out-of-area contact.

  • Family meeting locations

    Agreeing on places to meet near home and farther away, and making sure everyone knows them.

  • Emergency supply kits

    A grab-and-go kit and a stay-at-home kit, built for your household’s needs.

  • Heating and cooling during outages

    Staying safe in extreme heat or cold, and the dangers of using grills or stoves indoors.

  • Fire safety

    Working smoke alarms, extinguishers, escape plans, and practicing them as a family.
