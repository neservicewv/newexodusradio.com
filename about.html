About New Exodus Radio

New Exodus Radio was founded with a mission to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, strengthen the body of believers, and encourage spiritual and practical preparedness.

Revelation 18:4

Through biblical teaching, prayer, meaningful conversations, personal testimonies, and practical education, we seek to equip individuals and families for the challenges of today and the uncertainties of tomorrow.

We believe the Church is strongest when believers stand together in truth, humility, and love.

Our purpose is not simply to broadcast programs. It is to build a community of believers who are spiritually awake, prepared to endure, and committed to sharing the hope found in Jesus Christ.
